# 실사 액션 문구 사전

8대 그룹 순서로 정리했다. 애니 전용 단어는 넣지 않는다.

## 실사 고정
- "Live-action footage shot on a full-frame cinema camera with spherical primes, 24 fps, 180-degree shutter."
- "Real performers and stunt doubles on a practical set; dust, sparks and debris are practical effects captured in camera."
- "The impossible leap is a wire-assisted stunt captured in camera."
- "Pore-level skin, beads of sweat, flushed cheeks, flyaway hairs, natural catch-lights in both eyes."
- "Fabric has weight and lags half a beat behind the body."
- "Fine 35mm grain, mild lens breathing on moves, slight highlight bloom."

## TEMPORAL 시간
- "A sharp speed ramp: real time into 20% at the moment of contact, snapping back to real time." (샷 하나를 통째로 슬로모션으로 쓸 때는 하드컷으로 분리한다)
- "Shot at 120 fps and played back at 24 fps for this shot only."
- "Real-time action throughout except the single slow-motion shot."

## IMPACT 충격 정점
- "A single white flash frame on contact."
- "The frame shakes once on contact."
- "The impact peaks on a held frame of the fist buried in his jaw, then releases."

## KINETIC 힘 전달
- "The punch lands; his head snaps sideways, weight drops onto his back heel, knees buckle."
- "The kick drives him backward off his feet; his shoulders hit the wall first, then his head."
- "Her shoulder recoils from the impact and she resets her stance."
- "Momentum carries her through the turn into the next strike."
- "He is thrown over the table; it splits under his back."
- "A breakfall onto the concrete, palms slapping the ground."

## MOTION 움직임
- "Natural motion blur on the fist from a 180-degree shutter."
- "The arm overshoots past the target and snaps back to guard."
- "Hair and sleeves whip with the turn and settle after she stops."

## CAMERA 카메라
- "Handheld with 1-2 cm tremor, operator anchored, finding the action."
- "The camera jolts once on impact and settles."
- "A fast crash zoom onto his face as the blow lands."
- "Whip pan of at least 0.8 s with streaking background blur onto the next fighter."
- "Low Steadicam tracking at waist height, staying with her through the corridor."
- "Car-mounted camera at bumper height, road rushing at 90 km/h."
- "Over-the-shoulder 47° FOV, his shoulder blurred in the left foreground."

## VISUAL 시각 효과
- "Exposure spikes for a split second as the muzzle flashes."
- "Strobing police lights cut the corridor into flickers."
- "A brief heat-haze distortion behind the exhaust."

## EDITORIAL 편집
- "CUT on impact to the reverse angle of his reaction."
- "3.0s MATCH CUT ON ACTION — the punch is half-extended and still travelling screen-right at the cut."
- "The same kick lands twice from two angles: first wide, then a tight reverse." (오버래핑 액션, 씬당 1회)
- "Smash cut from the swing to the silent aftermath."

## AFTERMATH 여운
- "Dust and sweat spray off the impact and drift in the backlight."
- "Glass fragments rain down and settle on the floor."
- "The hanging bulb keeps swinging after the fight stops, shadows rocking."
- "He lies still; only his chest moves. Her breath fogs in the cold air."
- "A ringing silence, then the distant traffic returns."

## 총격·차량
- "Each shot has one muzzle flash, one ejected casing and visible recoil; seven rounds, then the slide locks open."
- "Brass casings bounce and roll across the tiles."
- "The car's suspension dips under braking, tires smoke and slide 2 meters, side mirror snaps off on the pillar."

## 사운드
- "Meaty body blows, cloth snaps, sharp exhales, shoe squeaks on concrete, room reverb. No music."
- "Every gunshot is a separate dry crack with a short echo tail, never a blended burst."
