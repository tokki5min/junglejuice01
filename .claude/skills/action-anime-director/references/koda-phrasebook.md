# Kōda (@aimikoda) 액션 문구 사전

연구 범위: 2026-08-26 ~ 2026-10-08 공개 게시물 156개(프롬프트 스레드 94개), 2026-10-12 정기 연구에서 10-09 ~ 10-12 추가, 2026-10-13에 10-12 칵테일 스레드 추가, 10-28(10-13 ~ 10-27)·11-11(10-28 ~ 11-10)에 이후 스레드 추가. 모델 비중은 Seedance 2.5와 MiniMax H3가 대부분이고 Wan 3.0이 일부다. 아래는 원문 그대로 인용한 문구다.

## 오프닝 / 엔딩
- "Start immediately in motion." / "Begin mid-movement" / "The scene opens already in motion"
- "Open with their strongest gesture already underway."
- "On the first frame, one fighter drops from above into the other's intercepting blade."
- "Cut mid-exchange with both still attacking and intensity rising."
- "End with both still travelling, hair and torn hems whipping."
- "End mid-exchange on their next violent collision, bodies still accelerating"

## 속도 / 스미어
- "Fast real-time sakuga combat: explosive travel, full torso and hip turns, extended limbs, deep foreshortening and directional smears resolving into clear anatomy at contact."
- "2D sakuga: compressed anticipation, explosive travel, deep foreshortening, parallax, smears, impact frames, hit-stops, recoil, immediate re-entry"
- "Long extended silhouettes snap into sharp folds."
- "Scratchy velocity strokes, directional trails, hair arcs"
- "brief directional smears and crisp readable silhouettes at the peak of each movement"
- "Full speed, strong foreshortening, brief smears, clear impacts and immediate recoil."
- "Accelerate between poses and brake sharply on each hold."
- "Contemporary dance body mechanics pushed to impossible speed: spiraling torsos, inverted extensions, scissoring legs and violent changes of axis."
- "She becomes a crimson-cyan streak and resolves higher between moving weapons."
- 부재로 속도 표현: "the camera catches only fragments, impacts and consequences, while the cat is already somewhere else."

## 금지 꼬리 (거의 모든 액션 프롬프트)
- "No slow motion, hit-stop, suspended staring, prolonged blade locks or repeated crouching silhouettes."
- "No slow motion, speed ramps, long windups or floating pauses."
- "Full-speed action throughout. No slow motion, frozen midair poses or gentle floating."
- "No extra weapons, duplicated limbs, costume changes or transformation into 3D."

## 타격 / 접촉
- "Carry parries into displacement and recoveries into attacks."
- "every reversal follows clear contact and consequence"
- "Show shot, impact, THEN fall."
- "Contact-driven camera jolts make the force felt; keep faces readable."
- "A violent crossing contact snaps directly into follow-through."
- "Glancing contact sends them apart on opposing arcs."
- "both rebound from a one-frame hit-stop" (히트스톱을 세어서 사용)
- "Black-white impact frame shears the skyline"
- "Hits have immediate local results; enemies resist until struck, defeated bodies stay down. No delayed cut lines, frozen victims, energy web or remote mass-kill pulse."
- "kills each opponent at actual blade contact, instantly launching toward the next"
- "Impacts should feel graphic and violent rather than explosive"
- "selective impact flashes keep movement readable without overwhelming effects"
- "The impacts land on consecutive heavy musical accents."

## 물리 / 파편 / 천
- "Swing pressure bends air; contacted rock peels into painted fragments, leaving gouges."
- "near-misses slice hair and sleeves" / "Her torn sleeve persists"
- "Abrupt starts and hard stops send visible force through shoulders, grip and clothing."
- "Rigid parts rotate; flexible materials bend or stretch."
- "Hair and robes lash behind acceleration."
- "Give the skirt substantial momentum: it follows her hips, overtakes her during abrupt stops and lashes around her during reversals."
- "no sliding, gliding or skidding. Sweeps use a planted foot; every landing grips the floor."
- "dark creature remains breaking apart into painterly shreds instead of realistic gore"
- "disintegrating into shredded ink ribbons and dry black dust ... crumbles into lifeless ash" (괴물 소멸, 머리는 마지막에: "Its horned head breaks apart last")
- 환경 피해를 다음 장애물로 재사용: "the missed claw splits a slender tree's base, leaving it leaning" → 다음 샷에서 괴물이 그 나무를 뽑아 퇴로를 막는다 → "She turns toward the thin end and commits to a vault"
- 부상 지속: "Her left shoulder clips the splintered trunk" 이후 "left arm held close", "injured shoulder low"; 괴물도 "its torn left wrist still sheds fibers"

## 소품 회계 / 연속 변형 (10-12 칵테일 스레드)
- 시작 상태 분리: "The images define appearance; the cocktail starts unprepared." / "No precut garnish." — 레퍼런스가 결과 상태를 미리 보여 주지 않게 한다.
- 배치 지도 한 단락: "Preparation area: empty clear martini glass at center; left, ...; right, ..." + "Objects stay where placed."
- 개수는 대문자로: "HALF a yellow lemon", "ONE thin half-moon slice", "ONE red fire beetle", "BOTH ear openings"
- 같은 물건 추적: "THAT SAME slice", "the same glass", "the same empty glass, still carrying the same half-moon lemon slice" + "It remains attached there through the final shot."
- 남은 물건도 위치를 다시 쓴다: "The remaining lemon half and knife stay on the board." / "the spoon is completely gone"
- 투명도로 가시성 확보: "Liquids remain translucent, keeping beetle and spoon visible." / "silver adds shimmer without opacity"
- 컷 없는 변형: "Show continuous material loss without a cut, sudden disappearance or concealing smoke." 변형은 단계로 쓴다(잠긴 부분 → 자루 → 손끝 마지막 방울) + 끝 상태 확인 "no handle or fragment remains", "Her visibly empty fingers open".
- 사운드로 컷 잇기: "Bar ambience bridges cuts, with knife taps, salt scrape, pours ..."
- 정적 후 터뜨리기: "Push in through one quiet beat. Smoke suddenly jets sideways ..." 직후 "A small, quick dolly back keeps all three sources visible"

- "No slow motion, no idle posing, no pauses between attacks. Every movement flows directly into another attack, parry or counterattack."

## Midjourney 습관
- sref를 여러 개 쌓고 profile을 1~2개 붙인다. 예: `--sref 1448908625 3123598145 387469134 3207844525 --profile kxxcnp9 --stylize 250`
