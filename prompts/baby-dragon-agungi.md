# CINEMATIC FANTASY | THE BABY DRAGON OF THE AGUNGI

Format: 16:9 | Duration: 25 seconds | Photorealistic CGI

## Scene & Atmosphere

Create a breathtaking, cinematic fantasy short set inside a humble Joseon-era Korean thatched-roof farmhouse kitchen (bueok) on a freezing winter dawn. The kitchen has rough earthen clay walls, exposed dark wooden beams blackened by years of smoke, a packed-earth floor, and a small wooden lattice window (salchang) covered with hanji paper, through which faint cold blue light filters from a snowy courtyard with snow-capped earthenware jars (onggi). At the center is a traditional Korean clay stove (buttumak) with a cold, dark firebox opening (agungi) at its base and a large black cast-iron cauldron (gamasot) sitting on top with a heavy wooden lid. A neat stack of split firewood, a bundle of dry rice straw, and a pile of dry pine needles lie beside the agungi. Cold blue winter light creates a beautiful contrast with the warm golden glow that gradually fills the kitchen. Use shallow depth of field, subtle handheld camera movement, realistic textures, fine film grain, and an intimate, heartwarming atmosphere.

## Main Characters

**The Woman:** A young Korean woman from the Joseon era with long, glossy black hair worn in a single long braid tied with a red daenggi ribbon. She wears a modest, slightly worn traditional hanbok: a quilted ivory cotton jeogori (padded winter jacket) over a deep indigo chima skirt, with a thick quilted vest (baeja). She shivers from the freezing temperature, her breath visible in the cold air, and her fingertips are red from the cold. Her expressions are gentle, natural, and full of warmth.

**The Baby Dragon:** An adorable, cat-sized baby East Asian dragon (Korean yong) with a long, slender, serpentine body like a small ferret, four short legs with tiny soft claws, and NO wings. Its pearly white scales shimmer with a soft pale jade and celadon iridescence, like Goryeo celadon porcelain. It has enormous round amber eyes, two tiny velvety deer-like antler buds, long delicate flowing whiskers, a soft wispy pale mane running along its spine, and a fluffy tuft at the tip of its long tail. It moves in gentle, floating, wave-like serpentine motions, sometimes drifting a few centimeters above the ground. Its movements are lifelike, innocent, playful, and emotionally expressive.

## Scene 1 | 0–6 Seconds: The Fire That Won't Catch

Begin with a cinematic wide shot of the freezing kitchen. The woman kneels on a straw mat in front of the cold agungi. She holds a piece of white flint (budsitdol) and strikes it with a small curved steel striker (busi). Bright orange sparks scatter onto a small bundle of dried mugwort tinder (budsitgit) in her palm, but they fade without catching. She tries again, sparks fly, and the tinder only smolders weakly and goes out. She sighs, blows warm breath into her cupped, trembling hands, her breath forming delicate clouds in the chilly air. From behind the woodpile, a tiny baby dragon slithers out in soft, wave-like motions, its long whiskers floating, and curiously approaches her. It climbs onto her knee, and the woman gently strokes its head. The dragon looks up at her with curious, sparkling amber eyes.

## Scene 2 | 6–12 Seconds: The Tiny Sneeze

Transition into an intimate close-up of the baby dragon's face as it peers at the falling sparks and the dead tinder. Its whiskers twitch, its eyes slowly close, its tiny nostrils flare, and it prepares to sneeze. Instead of fire, a small, soft puff of white misty cloud escapes from its nose and swirls away. The dragon opens its eyes in surprise, looking adorably confused and embarrassed, its little antler buds tilting. The woman smiles warmly, leans closer, points gently toward the dark agungi, and encourages it to try again. The dragon scrunches its face with determination, preparing for another sneeze.

## Scene 3 | 12–17 Seconds: A Spark of Magic

Capture a close two-shot from a low angle beside the agungi as the baby dragon suddenly sneezes again. This time, a tiny burst of golden-orange flame shoots from its mouth straight into the agungi and ignites the dry pine needles and rice straw kindling. The flames quickly spread to the split firewood and begin to dance, casting beautiful golden reflections across the earthen clay walls and the smoke-darkened beams. Soft wisps of steam begin to rise from beneath the lid of the iron cauldron above. The woman bursts into joyful laughter, clasping her hands as the warm firelight illuminates her face. The baby dragon looks at the newly lit fire with pride and innocent amazement, its whiskers glowing gold.

## Scene 4 | 17–25 Seconds: Warmth and Affection

The kitchen is now filled with a cozy, golden glow as the fire crackles gently inside the agungi and steam drifts from the cauldron. The woman carefully wraps the tiny dragon in a thick, soft quilted cotton blanket (nubi podaegi) with faded traditional patterns. The dragon coils into her lap, curls up comfortably, and nuzzles against her cheek. End with an emotional cinematic close-up of the woman gently touching her forehead to the dragon's snout while stroking its tiny head, its long whiskers softly brushing her cheek. The dragon purrs softly as they share a tender moment beside the glowing agungi.

## Cinematic Style & Sound Design

Ultra-realistic live-action aesthetic with photorealistic CGI, authentic Joseon-era Korean folk setting and costume, detailed iridescent dragon scales, delicate flowing whiskers and mane, believable serpentine creature animation, expressive eyes, natural human movements, cinematic composition, soft volumetric lighting through the hanji window and the rising steam, realistic fire and spark simulation, and smooth transitions. Begin with icy blue tones and gradually transition into rich amber and golden hues as the agungi comes alive.

Sound design includes howling winter wind outside the paper window, soft breathing, the sharp metallic clink of the steel striker against flint, tiny scraping dragon claws on the earthen floor, a cute misty sneeze, a gentle burst of flame, crackling firewood, and the soft bubbling of the cauldron. During the final scene, an emotional, gentle melody of gayageum and daegeum accompanied by delicate orchestral strings.

## Negative Prompt

No text, no watermark, no subtitles, no distorted faces, no extra limbs, no wings, no bat wings, no Western-style dragon, no dinosaur-like body, no malformed antlers, no unnatural movements, no flickering character designs, no inconsistent character appearance, no cartoon style, no plastic textures, no scary or aggressive dragon, no Japanese or Chinese architecture or clothing, no Western fireplace, no modern objects, no matches, no lighter, no sudden scene changes, and no unrealistic lighting.
